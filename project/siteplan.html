SmileCare Dental Clinic Site Plan

Planning document for a professional, responsive, and interactive dental clinic website.

Site Name

SmileCare Dental Clinic

The proposed website name is SmileCare Dental Clinic.

This name was selected because it communicates the clinic's focus on professional dental care while presenting a friendly and welcoming image. The word “Smile” connects directly with dental health, and “Care” emphasizes personal attention and patient well-being.

Possible domain: smilecaredentalclinic.com

The business name can be replaced with the client's official clinic name before the final website is published.

Site Purpose

The purpose of the website is to give current and potential patients clear information about the dental clinic, its professionals, services, location, schedule, and appointment process.

The website will also allow visitors to explore treatments, review frequently asked questions, save services of interest, and submit an appointment request from a mobile phone, tablet, or desktop computer.

Primary objectives

  • Present the clinic in a professional and trustworthy way.
  • Explain the dental treatments offered by the clinic.
  • Introduce the dentist and communicate professional experience.
  • Answer common patient questions before an appointment.
  • Make it easy to request an appointment.
  • Provide location, schedule, telephone, and contact information.
  • Encourage visitors to contact the clinic through WhatsApp.

Target Audience

Families

Parents and families looking for preventive and general dental care in a safe and welcoming environment.

Adults

Adults interested in oral health, dental cleaning, whitening, orthodontics, implants, or restorative procedures.

New Patients

People comparing dental clinics who need information about treatments, prices, schedules, location, and the dentist's experience.

User Scenarios

The website will be designed to answer questions that real patients may ask before choosing or contacting a dental clinic.

01

Services

What dental treatments does the clinic offer, and which treatment may be appropriate for my needs?

02

Appointments

How can I request an appointment, and what days and times are available?

03

Professional Experience

Who is the dentist, what qualifications do they have, and how much professional experience do they have?

04

Location

Where is the clinic located, what are its opening hours, and how can I contact it?

05

First Visit

What should I bring to my first dental appointment, and how should I prepare?

06

Emergency Care

What should I do if I have severe tooth pain, swelling, bleeding, or a broken tooth?

07

Costs and Payment

How much does an initial consultation cost, and what payment methods or insurance options are accepted?

08

Patient Confidence

What experiences have other patients had with the clinic and its treatments?

Typography

Heading Font

Montserrat

Professional Dental Care for Every Smile

Montserrat will be used for the site name, navigation, headings, buttons, and important calls to action. Its geometric design gives the website a modern and professional appearance.

Body Font

Roboto

Clear information helps patients make confident decisions about their dental care.

Roboto will be used for paragraphs, lists, form fields, service descriptions, frequently asked questions, and supporting text because it is highly readable on mobile and desktop screens.

Design and Accessibility Principles

Proximity

Related content, such as service names and descriptions, will be grouped together.

Alignment

Text, cards, buttons, and form fields will follow a consistent visual grid.

Repetition

Colors, buttons, spacing, typography, and card styles will be repeated across all pages.

Contrast

Dark text and strong buttons will appear against light backgrounds to support readability.

Accessibility

Semantic HTML, labels, alt text, keyboard focus indicators, sufficient contrast, and descriptive links will be included.

Responsive Design

The layout will adapt to portrait phones, landscape phones, tablets, laptops, and larger desktop screens.

Development Standards

  • The project will use original HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
  • At least three complete pages will share consistent navigation and visual styling.
  • All HTML and CSS will be validated before submission.
  • Images will be resized, compressed, and saved in modern formats when appropriate.
  • Images below the initial viewport will use lazy loading.
  • The website will use semantic HTML and accessible forms.
  • JavaScript output strings will exclusively use template literals.
  • The project will use more than one JavaScript function.
  • The project will use arrays, objects, array methods, conditions, events, DOM interaction, and localStorage.
  • Lighthouse will be tested for performance, accessibility, best practices, and SEO on mobile and desktop.
  • All external material will be cited on the references page.
